# Task 3: Risk Evaluation
**Project:** Risk Assessment & Mitigation Strategies  
**Organization:** SecureBank  

---

## Learning Objective
Evaluate risk treatment options using financial Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) and Return on Investment (ROI) metrics to support strategic cybersecurity investment decisions.

---

## Scenario Parameters
* **Current ALE:** $300,000
* **Implementation Cost:** $80,000 (one-time)
* **Annual Maintenance Cost:** $15,000 / year
* **Expected Risk Reduction:** 75% (0.75)

---

## Cost-Benefit Analysis

| Metric | Calculation | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Current ALE** | Given | **$300,000** |
| **New ALE after mitigation** | `$300,000 × (1 - 0.75)` | **$75,000** |
| **Annual Savings** | `$300,000 - $75,000` | **$225,000** |
| **3-Year Total Cost** | `$80,000 + ($15,000 × 3)` | **$125,000** |
| **3-Year Total Savings** | `$225,000 × 3` | **$675,000** |
| **Net Benefit** | `$675,000 - $125,000` | **$550,000** |
| **ROI** | `($550,000 / $125,000) × 100%` | **440%** |

---

## Decision & Justification

* **Decision:** **[X] Implement mitigation (cost-effective)**

* **Justification:**  
  Implementing the proposed mitigation yields an overwhelming financial return. Over a 3-year horizon, the control costs **$125,000** total but generates **$675,000** in loss avoidance savings. This produces a net financial benefit of **$550,000** and an outstanding Return on Investment (ROI) of **440%**. Furthermore, the solution pays for itself within less than 7 months of operation (Annual Savings of $225k vs. Year 1 Total Cost of $95k), making it a highly prudent and cost-effective security investment for SecureBank.
